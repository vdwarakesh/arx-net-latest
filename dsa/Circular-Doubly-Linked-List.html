Circular Doubly Linked List

A Circular Doubly Linked List is a type of circular linked list in which each node links to both its next and previous neighbors, while the first and last nodes are connected to each other. This creates a continuous loop that supports movement in either direction.

Imagine people seated around a circular table, where everyone knows both the person to their left and the person to their right. You can move clockwise or counterclockwise and continue around the table without reaching an endpoint.

Circular doubly linked lists are useful for music players with Next and Previous controls, carousel interfaces, rotating menus, and applications that cycle through items in both directions. The connections at both ends must be maintained whenever nodes are inserted or removed.


Summary

A Circular Doubly Linked List combines two-way navigation with a continuous loop, making it useful for collections that cycle forward and backward.
