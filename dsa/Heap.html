Heap

A Heap is a specialized tree-based data structure designed to keep the highest-priority or lowest-priority element readily accessible. It follows a specific ordering rule between connected elements, making it particularly useful when you need to repeatedly identify the next most important item.

Unlike a structure designed to keep every element fully sorted, a heap focuses on maintaining the relationship needed to identify its priority element efficiently. The remaining elements do not necessarily appear in complete sorted order.

Imagine an emergency room where patients are attended to according to urgency rather than arrival time. The hospital needs to identify the most urgent patient quickly without arranging every patient in a complete priority order. A heap follows a similar principle.


Where Is It Useful?

Heaps are used in task scheduling, event management, resource allocation, and systems that repeatedly need to select the most important item from a collection.


Summary

A heap is a priority-focused structure that keeps the most important element easy to access without requiring the entire collection to remain sorted.
